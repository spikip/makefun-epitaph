## About this entry

The epitaph opens with the most famous grave poem there is, "Do not
stand at my grave and weep", so nobody can mistake it for anything
but a tombstone line. The twist is that the dead speaker is not in
heaven or at peace: he is simply gone, because he finally left the
group chat. That is a small modern victory everyone recognises at
once, which is why the laugh lands on one read with no explanation
needed. The line stays in the voice of carved stone throughout, one
short sentence of dying speech, and the punchline is quotable enough
to repeat to a friend at a funeral or a dinner table. It stays well
inside the one hundred and fifty character cap. The program holds
the line, checks it against that cap, and writes it to
/output/entry.txt exactly as the bounty requires.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the one-line epitaph to `/output/entry.txt`.
